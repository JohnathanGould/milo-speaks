Meaning
Estoy is the first-person form of estar — used for location, temporary states, and feelings. Not the same as soy (ser), which is for identity.
Example
Estoy en el aeropuerto.
I am at the airport.
Mini tip
Estar vs Ser
Use estar for where you are and how you feel right now. Use ser for who you are.
Estoy cansado → I am tired (right now)
Soy canadiense → I am Canadian (always)
