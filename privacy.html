Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Milo Speaks Spanish collects, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Who We Are

Milo Speaks Spanish is operated by Johnathan Gould, a sole proprietor based in New Brunswick, Canada. Contact: johnathangould@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Display name — provided when you create an account
  • Email address — if you sign up with email and password
  • Google account information — if you sign in with Google, we receive your display name and profile photo URL from Google

Information we generate automatically

  • Learning progress — words you have studied, correct and incorrect answers, XP earned, streaks, bones, completed lessons and paths
  • Chat usage — the number of AI tutor messages you send per day (used for rate limiting only)
  • Community posts — content you post in La Plaza community chat

Information we do NOT collect

  • Payment information (the app is free)
  • Precise location data
  • Device identifiers beyond what Firebase provides automatically
  • Contact lists or other device data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Save and display your learning progress across sessions
  • Operate the leaderboard and community features
  • Enforce the daily AI tutor message limit
  • Improve the app and fix bugs

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising.

4. How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, hosted in the northamerica-northeast1 (Montreal, Canada) region. Firebase is operated by Google LLC. You can review Google's data practices at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

Milo Speaks Spanish is designed for general audiences and is family-friendly. The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at johnathangould@gmail.com and we will delete the account and associated data promptly.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at johnathangould@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Canadian residents (PIPEDA): You have the right to access your personal information and request corrections.

European residents (GDPR): If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights including the right to data portability and the right to object to processing.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.
